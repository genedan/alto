## Keyboard

| Keys | What it does |
|---|---|
| <kbd>1</kbd> to <kbd>9</kbd> | Press the matching answer button. A button's tooltip shows its number. Available when an exercise has nine answers or fewer and no separate octave row. |
| <kbd>Esc</kbd> | Go back from the statistics or database page. |
| <kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>Alt</kbd>+<kbd>S</kbd> | Open the app settings. |
| <kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>R</kbd> | Restart Alto. |
| <kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>Q</kbd> | Quit. |

On macOS, Qt shows these shortcuts with <kbd>⌘</kbd> in place of <kbd>Ctrl</kbd>.

## Mouse

| Action | Where | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| Click the staff | Note Construction | Place the note. |
| Right-click the staff | Note Construction with accidentals on | Step through no sign, sharp, flat and natural. |
| Double-click a row | Database: Answers or Questions | Preview that question. |
| Click a column heading | Database | Sort by that column. |
| Hover a chart | Statistics | Show the exact values for that period. |
| Drag the edge of the sidebar | Exercise screens | Resize the exercise list, or drag it narrower than its minimum to hide it. |

## The menu

The ☰ button at the top left holds **Home**, **Restart**, **Statistics**, **Browse database**, **Profile**,
**Settings…** and **Quit**.
