    API reference

    Every public module, class and function in Alto, with its signature and docstring. Names that begin with an underscore are internal and are left out. The pages are written from the docstrings by scripts/api_docs.py, so they match the code.

    alto

    Alto: a music theory trainer that learns what you find hard and asks it more often.

    Module What it is
    alto.anki_export Write portable Anki decks from Alto questions and optional calendar review dates.
    alto.audio Note playback: recorded instrument samples (University of Iowa), with pitch-shifting for missing notes.
    alto.circle_schemes The color schemes of the circle of fifths: the presets, and the schemes a user makes.
    alto.instrument_info What is known about each instrument’s recordings, for the summary shown in the instrument settings.
    alto.instrument_packs Media and fingerboard geometry supplied by third-party instrument packs.
    alto.location Where Alto keeps its practice history and its settings, and how to keep them somewhere else with ALTO_HOME.
    alto.notation_excerpts Public-domain piano excerpts available for offline notation practice.
    alto.notation_terms Notation Identification terms, grouped by subject with their score placement and SMuFL code points.
    alto.plugin_registry Install registered instrument packs from the latest release in their public GitHub repositories.
    alto.selfupdate Replacing a packaged copy of Alto with a newer release: whether it can, getting the update, checking it, and handing over.
    alto.settings User settings. Pure Python (no Qt); persistence lives in ui/exercise_settings.py.
    alto.terms Glossary entries that an installed plugin supplies, kept apart from the built-in ones.
    alto.updates What kind of copy of Alto is running, how it can be updated, and the commands and answers that checking involves.

    alto.exercises

    The exercises and the framework they are built on.

    Module What it is
    alto.exercises.base Exercise framework. Register a ChoiceExercise for button answers or an Exercise for construction tasks.
    alto.exercises.library The exercises: note names, intervals, key signatures, scales, chords, arpeggios, keyboard keys and note construction.
    alto.exercises.packs Load third-party instrument packs from ZIP archives and authoring folders.
    alto.exercises.rhythm_equations Build balanced rhythm equations with exact operator counts.

    alto.learning

    Spaced repetition: who is learning (profiles), what they answered (a database) and what to ask next.

    Module What it is
    alto.learning.backup Backup files: everything Alto keeps, in one file that can be read back into a clean install. Pure Python (no Qt).
    alto.learning.calendar Daily exercise queues for SM-2 and FSRS.
    alto.learning.database The SQLite store: profiles, every answer given, and what is known about each question. Pure Python (no Qt).
    alto.learning.grading Answer-button intervals for Anki’s default SM-2 and FSRS-6 deck settings.
    alto.learning.learner Ties the profile, the database and the scheduler together for the exercise screen.
    alto.learning.response_times Rolling response times for one learner and exercise.
    alto.learning.scheduler How a question’s history turns into how soon it comes back. Pure Python, no database and no Qt.
    alto.learning.stats Numbers and series for the statistics screen, worked out from the answers in the database. Pure Python, no Qt.

    alto.lessons

    The lessons: guided walks through a topic, with small exercises along the way.

    Module What it is
    alto.lessons.base What a lesson is made of: steps, and the small tasks inside them.
    alto.lessons.circle_of_fifths The lesson on the circle of fifths, taught from nothing.
    alto.lessons.clef_basics The lesson on clefs, taught from nothing.
    alto.lessons.duration A first rhythm lesson on counting the length of sound and silence.
    alto.lessons.intervals A guided introduction to naming intervals on the staff and keyboard.
    alto.lessons.key_signatures A guided lesson on reading and writing key signatures.
    alto.lessons.packs Turn the lessons that an instrument plugin declares into Lessons that Alto lists and shows.
    alto.lessons.pitches_and_notes A first lesson connecting a sound, a piano key, and a written note.
    alto.lessons.scale_identification Reading scales and recognizing their interval patterns.

    alto.theory

    Music theory, with no user interface.

    Module What it is
    alto.theory.fifths The circle of fifths: the twelve keys in order, each major with its relative minor.
    alto.theory.key_signatures Staff positions for written key signatures.
    alto.theory.pitch Pitches and intervals, spelled correctly (C# is not Db).
    alto.theory.scales Scales, modes, key signatures and chords, built from spelled intervals.

    alto.ui

    The windows, widgets and drawing code. Everything that needs Qt is in here.

    Module What it is
    alto.ui.anki_exporter Modeless Anki deck exporter using Alto’s exercise controls and music engraving.
    alto.ui.answer_bank A responsive, scrollable grid for large banks of answer buttons.
    alto.ui.app_settings The application-wide settings dialog.
    alto.ui.background Run slow work, such as a download, on another thread, and hear how it went on the window’s thread.
    alto.ui.backup_flow Exporting a backup and importing one: the file dialogs, the questions asked, and the safety copy made before anything is replaced.
    alto.ui.charts Small charts drawn with QPainter, in the application’s theme colors.
    alto.ui.circle The circle of fifths: twelve wedges, the major keys on the outside and their relative minors on the inside.
    alto.ui.circle_colors The pages of the circle of fifths settings that choose its colors: a grid of schemes to pick from, and an editor to make one.
    alto.ui.colors Mixing colors. The interface blends palette colors to get its soft tints and highlights.
    alto.ui.database_page A read-only look at what is stored: every answer, every question’s record, and the profiles.
    alto.ui.degree_text Draw scale-degree carets above their digits without relying on font-specific combining accents.
    alto.ui.dropdown Shared dropdown fields with rounded popup windows.
    alto.ui.engraving Drawing notation with SMuFL music fonts (Bravura, Leland, Petaluma, MuseJazz and others).
    alto.ui.exercise_settings Load and save each exercise’s settings.
    alto.ui.exercise_view The exercise screen: a question, its answer buttons, the score, and a pane of settings that slides in from the right.
    alto.ui.fingerboard Render a pack’s instrument portrait using its calibrated string endpoints.
    alto.ui.glossary A searchable glossary of the notation terms used in Alto.
    alto.ui.home The start screen: a card for every exercise, or for every lesson, in rows.
    alto.ui.icons Small monochrome icons drawn from SVG, tinted with the current theme’s text color.
    alto.ui.instrument_picker Choosing the instrument that plays notes, with a summary of what each one is made from.
    alto.ui.interval_range A two-handle slider for selecting interval distances without changing the keyboard range.
    alto.ui.key_fork The fork: a picker for a key signature, shaped like a tuning fork lying on its side.
    alto.ui.keyboard The on-screen piano keyboard, drawn to real proportions, and a miniature of the whole piano for choosing a span of keys.
    alto.ui.keyboard_styles Keyboard styles: the colors and materials the on-screen piano keys are drawn in.
    alto.ui.lesson The lesson pages: a page that walks through one lesson step by step.
    alto.ui.lesson_nav The list on the left in lesson mode: the lessons, and the steps of the one that is open.
    alto.ui.main_window The main window: tile browsing or a workspace with an independently selected exercise or lesson list.
    alto.ui.meter_staff Staffless rhythm notation for lesson and glossary examples.
    alto.ui.metronome_spectrum A bouncing spectrum display for the metronome preview.
    alto.ui.midi_input ALSA keyboard input for answering a question with a played pitch.
    alto.ui.motion Animations and feedback delays, run in one place so that tests decide when they happen.
    alto.ui.notation Notation styles: which music font the staff, clefs, notes and accidentals are engraved in.
    alto.ui.notes Engraved notes built from the Noto Music font’s glyphs, the way notation software draws them.
    alto.ui.pack_settings_panel The settings panels of exercises that plugins add.
    alto.ui.package_update The window that updates a packaged copy of Alto: the download and its checks with their progress, then what happens next.
    alto.ui.pictures Small pictures of musical things (clefs, note values, key signatures, note ranges …) for the settings panes.
    alto.ui.plugins_dialog The Plugins dialog: the installed plugins on the left, and the buttons that add or remove one on the right.
    alto.ui.practice_limits Daily card allowances in profile and exercise settings.
    alto.ui.profile_dialog Choosing who is practicing. There is no password: a profile is just a name that keeps its own history.
    alto.ui.question_preview A popup showing a stored question the way the exercise showed it, with its answer.
    alto.ui.reference The reference pane: an interactive circle of fifths to look things up on, with an explanation under it.
    alto.ui.response_times Profile speed-grading settings and the rolling distribution for each exercise.
    alto.ui.scale_explorer Interactive notation and step patterns for learning scales.
    alto.ui.score_excerpt Paint the engraved score and locate symbols from a pixel map made from the same SVG.
    alto.ui.settings_menu The building blocks of the settings panels that slide in beside an exercise.
    alto.ui.settings_panel The settings panels of the built-in exercises, and settings_panel_for, which picks the panel for any exercise.
    alto.ui.staff The staff: lines, clefs, key signatures and notes, drawn on a rounded white “paper”.
    alto.ui.staff_input The staffs you click to place notes or a clef.
    alto.ui.staff_playback Connect a displayed music sample to the notes sounding on its staff.
    alto.ui.stats_dialog The statistics screen: a few numbers and charts about how practice is going.
    alto.ui.store The application’s saved settings (a thin layer over QSettings).
    alto.ui.style Application-wide stylesheet: rounded corners on buttons, panes, lists and scroll bars.
    alto.ui.theme The color theme: follow the system, force light or dark, or use a theme with colors of its own.
    alto.ui.theme_cards The card of a theme in the settings.
    alto.ui.theme_editor The editor for a theme of the user’s own: a name, and a color for every part of its light and dark versions.
    alto.ui.transition_preview A preview of a transition for the settings.
    alto.ui.transitions The transitions between the exercises and the lessons on the home page.
    alto.ui.update_flow Checking for a newer Alto and installing it: the questions asked, the progress shown, and the restart afterwards.
    alto.ui.warmup Get ready, while Alto sits idle just after it opens, what a first visit to a page would otherwise pay for.
    alto.ui.widgets Small widgets shared by several screens: the sidebar toggle, the help button and a segmented control.
    alto.ui.zoom Zoom: make everything in Alto larger or smaller together.
