    Shortcuts

    Keyboard

    For keyboard practice in Notation Identification, select Question Type → Answer Button. When you choose an exercise from the list, the keyboard goes to it, so its keys work at once. Tab and Shift+Tab move between the buttons, and Space presses the one that has the focus. Hover over any button to see its key.

    Choosing an answer

    Keys What it does
    1 to 9 Press the matching answer button, counting from the left. Available when an exercise has nine answers or fewer.
    C D E F G A B Keyboard Note Identification and Circle of Fifths: the natural note or key of that name. A minor key is typed as the major key of its letter.
    Shift+a letter, Ctrl+a letter Keyboard Note Identification and Circle of Fifths: the sharp (Shift) or the flat (Ctrl) of that note or key.
    1 to 6 Circle of Fifths, Roman numerals: I, ii, iii, IV, V and vi. For a key with two names (such as B and C♭) press the key of each name.
    1 to 9 Keyboard Note Identification with the octave asked: the octave, typed as its number.
    A S D F Arpeggio Identification: the inversion, which is the second row of buttons. The chord type is on 1 to 4.

    With scale-degree answers, digits name the degrees. In a bank with more than nine choices, add Shift for a sharp or Ctrl for a flat; add Alt as well for a double sharp or double flat. The octave row uses A, S, D, F and so on, as shown on its buttons.

    Staff placement in Note, Interval, and Scale Construction

    Clicking the staff is never needed. A pale note, the caret, shows the note that would be placed, and the line under the staff says what comes next, in words that a screen reader can read.

    Keys Button What it does
    ↑ / ↓ Higher / Lower Move the note one line or space up or down. The first press starts from the middle line of the staff.
    Page Up / Page Down Octave ↑ / Octave ↓ Move the note an octave up or down.
    A to G   Jump to the nearest note with that name, above or below.
    ← / →   Step backwards or forwards through no sign, ♯, ♭ and ♮ (♮ only when the staff has a key signature).
    + - 0 ♯ ♭ ♮ Choose a sharp, a flat or a natural for the next note.
    Return Place note Place the note at the caret.
    Backspace Undo Scale Construction: take back the last note or step.

    Interval Construction also accepts Ctrl+- for a double flat and Ctrl++ for a double sharp.

    Once all the notes of a scale are placed, and the Steps setting is on, the same keys mark the steps:

    Keys Button What it does
    W / H W / H Choose a whole or a half step.
    ↑ ↓ ← →   Switch between whole and half.
    Return Mark step Mark the step and move on to the next pair of notes.

    When everything is placed, Return presses Answer. Return always does the next thing: it places a note or marks a step while there is something to place, and answers once there is not.

    Building a key signature (Key Signature Construction)

    Keys What it does
    ↑ or → One more sharp (or one fewer flat).
    ↓ or ← One more flat (or one fewer sharp).
    Home Back to the natural sign: no sharps or flats.
    Return Answer.

    Placing a clef (Clef Basics, Clef Construction)

    Keys What it does
    ← or → Choose the G, C or F clef before or after this one.
    ↑ or ↓ Move it up or down a line or space.
    Return Answer: place it where it is.

    Everywhere

    Keys What it does
    Esc Go back from the statistics or database page.
    Ctrl+Alt+S Open the app settings.
    Ctrl+R Restart Alto.
    Ctrl+Q Quit.
    Ctrl++ Zoom in. Ctrl+= does the same, so no Shift is needed.
    Ctrl+- Zoom out.
    Ctrl+0 Go back to 100%.

    On macOS, Qt shows these shortcuts with ⌘ in place of Ctrl.

    Zoom makes everything larger or smaller together: the text, the buttons, the lists, the notation, the keyboard and the icons. It moves through 50%, 67%, 75%, 80%, 90%, 100%, 110%, 125%, 150%, 175%, 200%, 250% and 300%. Alto draws the window again at each step, and remembers the zoom the next time it starts. The current percentage briefly appears to the left of the Glossary button. Windows that are already open, such as the settings, take the new zoom when you open them again.

    Mouse

    Action Where What it does
    Click the staff Note, Interval, and Scale Construction Place the note.
    Right-click the staff Note Construction, Scale Construction Step through no sign, sharp, flat and natural.
    Turn the wheel over the staff Scale Construction Step through the signs while notes are placed, then switch between whole and half steps.
    Double-click a row Database: Answers or Questions Preview that question.
    Click a column heading Database Sort by that column.
    Hover a chart Statistics Show the exact values for that period.
    Turn the wheel with Ctrl held Anywhere Zoom in or out one step for each click of the wheel.
    Drag the edge of the sidebar Exercise screens Resize the exercise list, or drag it narrower than its minimum to hide it.

    The menu

    Open the ☰ menu at the top left for Profile, Settings…, Tools, Help, Restart, or Quit. Under Help, About shows the installed version and development status. Report an issue opens Alto’s GitHub issues page. Under Tools, choose Import backup…, Export backup…, Anki Exporter, or Check for updates…. For deck selection, scheduling, and import instructions, see Anki decks.
