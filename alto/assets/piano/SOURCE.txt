Piano samples: Salamander Grand Piano V3, by Alexander Holm.
A Yamaha C5 grand piano recorded with two AKG C414 microphones in an AB position about 12 cm above the strings
(48 kHz, 24-bit, 16 velocity layers, sampled in minor thirds from the lowest A).
https://sfzinstruments.github.io/pianos/salamander   https://github.com/sfzinstruments/SalamanderGrandPiano

Changes made for Alto: layer 8 of 16 (medium) only, the 30 recordings (MIDI 21, 24, 27 ... 108, A0 to C8), mixed to mono and
converted to 44.1 kHz 16-bit, the silence before each note trimmed, cut to 2.5 s with a 0.5 s fade-out, and levelled (first-second
RMS 0.0915, peaks under 0.8). File names are MIDI note numbers. Alto pitch-shifts the nearest recording, by at most a semitone,
for the notes in between.

Every file's pitch was checked against its name: A0 to C5 are within about 20 cents of equal temperament, and the notes above
rise to 60 cents sharp at C8, the stretch tuning of a real piano. This is left as recorded.
